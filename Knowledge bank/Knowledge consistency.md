# Knowledge consistency

This is the canonical policy for materially duplicated and contradictory vault knowledge. `AGENTS.md` remains authoritative for permissions and safety. The project skill at `.agents/skills/audit-knowledge-consistency/SKILL.md` implements this policy.

## Scope

The goal is one coherent living body of knowledge, not an archive of overlapping summaries. Similar wording is not itself duplication. Orientation and operating surfaces may repeat a compact claim when that repetition is necessary for the surface to work.

This is not a freshness policy. Note age, missing dates, and elapsed time are not findings and never make one claim win. Date can identify which version or environment a claim describes.

## Duplicate knowledge

Knowledge is materially duplicated when locations communicate substantially the same reusable claim, rationale, or procedure without distinct purposes that justify repetition.

When duplication is in scope:

1. Choose the clearest existing subject note as canonical home.
2. Merge unique qualifiers, useful evidence, provenance, and links.
3. Rewrite and compress rather than appending another summary.
4. Update affected wikilinks and recheck old claim wording.
5. Treat whole-note removal according to the authorization boundary in `AGENTS.md`.

Do not classify an orientation summary linked to detail, a quotation plus interpretation, clearly separated findings and hypotheses, scoped project orientation, or claims about different authorities, versions, environments, dates, or scopes as duplicates without further evidence.

## Contradictions and scoped differences

A contradiction exists only when claims cannot both be true under compatible meanings, authority, version, environment, date, and scope. Claims about different versions, environments, dates, or scopes are scoped differences, not contradictions. Make the distinction explicit in the relevant notes rather than calling it a conflict or choosing a winner.

An actual contradiction may be resolved automatically only when there is:

- explicit supersession by the same authority; or
- a verified transcription or citation error.

Recency alone never proves supersession. Preserve rationale and provenance when they affect trust.

Every other contradiction requires human review. Add or update one entry in [[conflicts]] with the topic, competing claims, evidence, needed decision, and open status. Do not create duplicate entries. After the user resolves it, update the living notes and remove the queue entry.

## Permission modes

- **Ordinary writing:** search first and integrate into an existing subject note. It does not authorize a vault-wide audit or consolidation.
- **Consistency audit:** runs only when explicitly requested and is read-only. It reports duplicate and contradiction candidates with evidence and proposed treatment.
- **Consistency consolidation:** runs only for an explicitly requested scope. It may make safe merges and the narrow automatic corrections above, but does not by default authorize ambiguous conflict resolution, whole-note deletion, substantial deletion, or broad restructuring.

Routine conversation, schedules, note age, missing dates, and elapsed time never trigger an audit or consolidation.

## Completion

A consistency pass searches every in-scope note, classifies each candidate as duplicate, complementary, intentional repetition, scoped difference, explicit supersession, contradiction, or uncertain, preserves unique useful knowledge in merges, surfaces each ambiguous contradiction once, rechecks links and old wording, and reports changed notes and unresolved decisions.

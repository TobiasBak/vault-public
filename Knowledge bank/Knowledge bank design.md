# Knowledge bank design

This public vault is a subject-centered, living Markdown knowledge bank for reusable understanding from work, experiments, research, project thoughts, agent histories, and personal memory that is suitable for public sharing. Notes are human-readable, connected with meaningful Obsidian `[[wikilinks]]`, and free to evolve without mandatory frontmatter, schemas, or note types. Folders and indexes earn their place through use.

## Working knowledge

The bank preserves conclusions that can improve future understanding, decisions, or action. Search before creating, integrate into an existing subject note when possible, and compress as understanding improves. Keep notes understandable without hidden chat context, but do not preserve the research trail merely to prove each claim.

The concision budget for always-loaded agent instructions and active context does not apply to ordinary retrievable knowledge. `AGENTS.md`, skills, and working context pay a recurring attention cost; a living note is loaded when relevant and may be as detailed as needed to preserve meaning, rationale, examples, conditions, and recovery paths. Compress duplication and narration, not the distinctions needed to understand, verify, or safely apply the knowledge.

Stored claims express current working knowledge and remain usable until Tobias, another agent, new evidence, or changed conditions challenge them. A challenge triggers verification and revision. Proof, source inventories, confidence labels, methodology, and exhaustive alternatives are not default note elements.

Selective provenance matters when authority affects trust, a claim is volatile or disputed, exact measurements define the finding, conflict resolution depends on it, or error would be consequential. Optimization knowledge retains baseline, intervention, effect, conditions, and tradeoffs because those details make the result meaningful. Repositories and other external systems remain authoritative for changing implementation detail.

## What earns a place

Keep knowledge when it could materially improve a future agent's understanding, decision, or action and is not so generic or cheap to rediscover that storage adds little. Useful knowledge includes findings, corrections, preferences, rationale, important failures, established procedures, and decision-relevant hypotheses worth carrying forward. A hypothesis belongs when its uncertainty changes future reasoning; an untested implementation or experiment design belongs only to active project work.

Distill work into the smallest useful change in understanding or behavior, not the shortest wording. Preserve scope, conditions, rationale, examples, provenance or retrieval routes, and tradeoffs when they determine meaning, applicability, verification, or safe recovery. Omit the surrounding research process and details that do not change understanding or a decision. A direct claim is preferable to a report explaining how it was produced only while the claim remains specific and recoverable.

Research is input to the bank, not its default output. Active projects may hold plans and untested experiments while they are being pursued; the living bank receives their reusable conclusions after they become useful knowledge. Raw session histories remain in their source systems and are not mirrored here. A conclusion enters the bank only when it is worth keeping and can be integrated into its owning subject note.

The operating permissions, writing workflow, safety rules, and orientation behavior live in [[AGENTS]]. Material duplication and contradiction handling live in [[Knowledge consistency]]. This note explains why the bank is lightweight rather than repeating those contracts.

## Design roots

[Karpathy's LLM Wiki](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) supplies the core idea of an agent-maintained, interlinked Markdown wiki built from durable sources while remaining implementation-agnostic.

[Obsidian Second Brain](https://github.com/eugeniughelbur/obsidian-second-brain) demonstrates a much larger design. The useful roots here are search before creation, notes independent of hidden conversation, selective provenance, active duplicate consolidation, surfaced contradictions, and human judgment over agent bookkeeping. Its schemas, folder layout, freshness rules, commands, hooks, and scheduled automation are optional implementation choices, not requirements for this vault.

# Knowledge bank design

This public Markdown vault holds reusable knowledge from work, experiments, research, projects, agent histories, and personal memory suitable for sharing. Notes center on subjects, use human-readable names and meaningful Obsidian `[[wikilinks]]`, and evolve without mandatory frontmatter, schemas, or note types. Folders and indexes earn their place through use.

## Working knowledge

Keep conclusions that improve future understanding, decisions, or action. Search before creating, integrate into an existing subject note where possible, and compress as understanding improves. Notes must make sense without hidden chat context; they need not retain a research trail for every claim.

Always-loaded instructions, skills, and active context pay a recurring attention cost. Retrievable notes do not share that concision budget: keep the meaning, rationale, examples, conditions, and recovery paths they need. Cut duplication and narration, not distinctions needed to understand, verify, or safely apply the knowledge.

Stored claims are current working knowledge until Tobias, another agent, new evidence, or changed conditions challenge them. Then verify and revise. Proof, source inventories, confidence labels, methodology, and exhaustive alternatives are not default note elements.

Keep provenance when authority, volatility, dispute, exact measurements, conflict resolution, or consequential errors make it useful. For optimizations, retain the baseline, change, result, conditions, and tradeoffs. Repositories and external systems own changing implementation details.

## What earns a place

Findings, corrections, preferences, rationale, important failures, established procedures, and useful hypotheses belong when they can change future reasoning or action. Omit generic or cheaply rediscoverable detail. Keep a hypothesis when its uncertainty matters; untested implementations and experiment designs belong only to active project work.

Distill the smallest useful change in understanding or behavior, not the shortest wording. Preserve scope, conditions, rationale, examples, provenance, retrieval routes, and tradeoffs when they affect meaning, use, verification, or recovery. Cut the surrounding research process. State conclusions directly while keeping them specific and recoverable.

Research feeds the bank; reusable conclusions are its output. Active projects may retain plans and untested experiments while pursuing them. Raw sessions stay in their source systems. Promote a conclusion only when it is worth keeping in its owning subject note.

[[AGENTS]] owns permissions, writing workflow, safety, and orientation. [[Knowledge consistency]] owns material duplication and contradictions. This note explains the lightweight design.

## Design roots

[Karpathy's LLM Wiki](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) supplies the core idea of an agent-maintained, interlinked Markdown wiki built from durable sources while remaining implementation-agnostic.

[Obsidian Second Brain](https://github.com/eugeniughelbur/obsidian-second-brain) demonstrates a much larger design. The useful roots here are search before creation, notes independent of hidden conversation, selective provenance, active duplicate consolidation, surfaced contradictions, and human judgment over agent bookkeeping. Its schemas, folder layout, freshness rules, commands, hooks, and scheduled automation are optional implementation choices, not requirements for this vault.

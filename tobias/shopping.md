# Shopping preferences

- Footwear: fit and comfort matter more than discounts. Price still matters; rank suitable fits first, then compare deals.
- Match fit evidence to the area where a shoe feels restrictive. Toe-box measurements do not establish midfoot or instep fit; do not rank shoes for those areas from toe-box data alone.
- Recommend products that can actually be bought and delivered in Denmark. Verify the requested variant's availability, not just the product listing.
